# Folio Design Principle: No Sparkles Rule

## Strict Policy
**Never use sparkles or sparkle icons (`Sparkles`) anywhere on FOLIO.**

If any sparkle icon is found, proposed, or considered for the website, it must be replaced with a purpose-driven, context-specific minimalist icon:

| Context | Replacement Icon |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Analytics & Data Insights** | `TrendingUp`, `Activity`, `Compass`, or `Clock` |
| **Classics & Recommended Books** | `BookOpen`, `Bookmark`, `Library`, or `Award` |
| **Session Completion & Rituals** | `Award`, `CheckCircle2`, `Flame`, or `Clock` |
| **Focus Mode & Timer Initiation** | `Timer`, `Flame`, `Zap`, or `Play` |
| **App Launcher & Offline Capabilities** | `Compass`, `Rocket`, `Zap`, or `Laptop` |
| **Web Reader & Sample Articles** | `Compass`, `Globe`, `Bookmark`, or `ExternalLink` |

## Visual Rationale
Folio adheres to an **Apple Calm luxury & editorial typography** aesthetic. Sparkles signal gimmicky AI magic or gamified noise. Folio favors calm, literary, and functional iconography with clear semantic purpose.
